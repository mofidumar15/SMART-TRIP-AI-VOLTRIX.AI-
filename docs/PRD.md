# SmartTrip AI Product Requirements

## Problem

Planning a group trip currently requires multiple disconnected apps.

- WhatsApp
- Splitwise
- Booking Apps
- Google Maps
- Notes

## Solution

One AI-powered collaborative platform.

## Target Users

- College students
- Friend groups
- Families
- Corporate teams

## MVP

- Authentication
- Create Trip
- Invite Members
- AI Itinerary
- Group Voting
- Expense Tracking
- Shared Dashboard
- AI Assistant

## Future

- Guide Marketplace
- Partner Integrations
- Smart Recommendations
- Premium Plans
